Final Conclusion¶

The Geldium Credit Delinquency analysis was conducted to identify customers who are at higher risk of payment delinquency and to understand the major factors associated with delinquent behaviour.

The analysis identified important risk indicators such as missed payments, credit score, credit utilization, loan balance, income and debt-to-income ratio. Approximately 16% of customers in the dataset were delinquent, highlighting the importance of proactive risk monitoring.

The key business solution is to implement a risk-based collection strategy. Customers showing multiple risk signals should be prioritized for early intervention, while medium-risk customers can be monitored through reminders and regular follow-ups. Low-risk customers can continue under the standard collection process.

The analysis can further be enhanced by developing a machine learning model that predicts the probability of delinquency for each customer. This would allow Geldium to move from reactive collection to proactive, data-driven risk management.

Overall, the proposed solution can help Geldium improve collection prioritization, identify risky customers earlier, optimize collection resources and support better credit-risk management.
